# CTF Write-Up

Use this skill after a candidate is Accepted and clean reproduction has passed, or when reviewing a generated write-up.

## Rules

- Use only recorded facts and artifacts.
- Do not invent commands, endpoints, offsets, payloads, or failed paths.
- Include failed approaches only when they explain the final solution.
- Verify final flag, solver command, and evidence paths against records.
- Redact secrets before publishing.
