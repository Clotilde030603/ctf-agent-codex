# CTF Crypto Math

Use this skill for number theory, algebra, lattice, RSA, ECC, or protocol-parameter challenges.

## Focus

- List known parameters and unknown variables.
- Distinguish encoding, hashing, encryption, signatures, and key exchange.
- Estimate search space before brute force.
- Prove the mathematical weakness and reproduce the plaintext or flag.
